# Assorted Bags

Adds bags you carry around and open anywhere. A plain bag can be dyed with two colors and upgraded through every storage material, and the ender bag opens your ender chest from wherever you are.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

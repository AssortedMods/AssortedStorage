# Assorted Barrels

Adds barrels in many materials, from stone to netherite, that hold far more than a vanilla barrel and open without needing space above them. Level upgrades can raise a barrel one material at a time without emptying it.

A padlock turns a vanilla barrel into a locked one that only the matching key opens.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

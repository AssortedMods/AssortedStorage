# Assorted Chests

Adds chests in many materials, from stone to netherite, that hold far more than a vanilla chest. Level upgrades can raise a chest one material at a time without emptying it.

The locked chest and locked ender chest are in [Assorted Locks](../locks).

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod. Locked chests need Assorted Locks installed as well.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

# Assorted Chests

Adds chests in many materials, from stone to netherite, that hold far more than a vanilla chest. Level upgrades can raise a chest one material at a time without emptying it.

A padlock turns a vanilla chest or ender chest into a locked one that only the matching key opens. Locked ender chests share their storage with every ender chest on the same combination.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

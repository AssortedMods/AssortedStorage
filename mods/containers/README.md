# Assorted Containers

Adds storage that looks good in a room. Wood and glass cabinets, gold and obsidian safes, a locker that stacks two tall, item towers you can build as high as you like, and a warehouse crate for every kind of wood.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

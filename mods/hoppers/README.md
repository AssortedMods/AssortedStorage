# Assorted Hoppers

Adds hoppers in many materials. They move items the same way the vanilla hopper does, but hold far more and the better materials move items faster. A stone one is the smallest and a netherite one the largest. With Assorted Chests installed you can also make one from a chest of the same material. With Assorted Locks you can put a padlock on a vanilla hopper to lock it.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod. Locked hoppers need Assorted Locks installed as well.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

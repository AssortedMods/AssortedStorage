# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release as its own mod. It used to only be part of Assorted Storage
- Worlds from Assorted Storage 11.x keep all of their level upgrades
- Level upgrades are now crafted from eight of their material around a sheet of paper
- The old recipe around a blank upgrade still works when Assorted Crates is installed
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0

# Assorted Level Upgrades

Adds level upgrades. Use one on a chest, barrel, hopper or shulker box and it becomes the next storage material up without being emptied. Each upgrade is crafted from eight of its material around a sheet of paper. Vanilla containers can be upgraded too once the Assorted Storage mod that adds their material versions is installed, and storage crates take them for extra room.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

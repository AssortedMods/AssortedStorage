# Assorted Locks

Adds padlocks, keys, key rings and the locksmith workbench. Give a lock and a key the same combination at the workbench and only that key opens what the lock is on. Every vanilla door can be locked, and so can the doors from Assorted Building Blocks. Vanilla chests, ender chests, barrels, hoppers and shulker boxes take a padlock and keep what is inside. A locked ender chest opens the ender storage of its combination. With the other Assorted Storage mods installed their chests, barrels, hoppers, shulker boxes and crates take a padlock too.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

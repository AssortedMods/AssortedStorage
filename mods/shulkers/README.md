# Assorted Shulkers

Adds shulker boxes in many materials. They work like the vanilla shulker box and keep their contents when broken, but hold far more. A stone one is the smallest and a netherite one the largest. They can be dyed like the vanilla ones. With Assorted Locks you can also put a padlock on a vanilla shulker box to lock it.

Also included in [Assorted Storage](../storage). Worlds made with Assorted Storage 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod. Locked shulker boxes need Assorted Locks installed as well.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
